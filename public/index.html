About (me)

私について

I photograph the space between moments — the pause before someone laughs, the hour the city forgets itself, the way paper holds the morning.

Born in Kanazawa and trained in Tokyo, I have spent the last decade working with editors, brands and couples who value restraint over spectacle. I shoot mostly on medium-format film and natural light.
